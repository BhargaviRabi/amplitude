## Summary / Explanation

This learning path walks through Amplitude's three main collaboration surfaces, each solving a different sharing problem. **Dashboards** (Part 1) collect related charts into one place so a team can monitor important metrics "at a glance" — supported by pre-built Starter Templates and the ability to build your own reusable Dashboard templates. **Notebooks** (Part 2) go a step further than a Dashboard by letting you add written context and explanation around Charts, Cohorts, and other assets — the Knowledge Check in Part 3 directly tests this distinction (Question 4: when you need both the data *and* narrative context for a colleague, a Notebook — not a bare Dashboard — is the right tool). **Spaces** (Part 3) are the broadest container: a shared home where Dashboards, Notebooks, Charts, Cohorts, and even other Spaces can all live together for a team or project, and where external sharing is done via public links rather than ad hoc chart-by-chart link sharing. The Knowledge Check also clarifies a subtle Spaces mechanic: the same Dashboard can appear in two different Spaces via a shortcut in one and storage in the other, rather than needing to be duplicated. Together, the three parts form an escalating toolkit: Dashboards for at-a-glance metrics, Notebooks for narrated analysis, and Spaces for organizing and sharing all of it with a team.
